Preparing audio

How it works

  1. Put the phone or laptop in the middle of the table and press Start recording. Keep this page open; the screen stays on while recording.
  2. Press Stop. The app finds where each voice speaks, then transcribes the speech.
  3. Rename “Speaker 1”, “Speaker 2” … to real names, then copy or download the transcript.

The first run downloads the speech models (about 240 MB with Fast) and keeps them in the browser for next time. Only the models come from the internet; your recording stays here.
